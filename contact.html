Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a local service?

Visit the Services page, select the service you need, choose a provider, and complete the booking process.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. You can manage your bookings from your Bookings section and cancel an eligible booking when required.

How can I become a service provider?

How can I submit feedback?

You can submit your experience by visiting the Reviews page and writing a review for your completed service.
